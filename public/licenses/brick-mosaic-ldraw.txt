The part/colour facts above are a curated finite subset; the LEGO website,
product artwork and trademarks are not described as licensed under CC BY 4.0.
LEGO design ID, colour ID and element ID are separate identifiers. LDraw colour
codes are a fourth system and are not BrickLink colour IDs. Several element IDs
can refer to the same design/colour combination; the tool uses observed IDs
rather than constructing them from design and colour numbers.

RGB values and the drawn stud preview are digital approximations. LDraw RGB
values can differ from the manufacturer's own digital swatches, and neither is
a promise of exact physical colour. A catalogue listing establishes an observed
part/colour combination, not current stock, price, delivery or continued
production. The tool does not promise BrickLink import compatibility, structural
strength or wall-mount safety. Nominal stud pitch is approximately 8 mm, derived
from the LDraw specification's 20 LDU at approximately 0.4 mm per LDU:
https://www.ldraw.org/article/218.html
