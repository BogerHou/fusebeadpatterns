## Reproduce the browser exports

The checked-in sample downloads are real generator exports, not recreated illustrations.
On 8 October 2026, the source above was opened in the local Chrome generator using
Perler Midi (all 103 colors enabled), Midi 29 × 29, DeltaE CIE2000, no dithering,
brightness/contrast/saturation 100, grayscale 0, Center and Fit To Boards on.
No bead edits or source retouching were made. Both projects were then reopened and
exported with **Use Symbols In Printable Exports** checked; their RGBA grids were
verified unchanged. Their saved export file names end in `-symbols`, while the
public download names below stay short.

- 1 × 1 boards: `cat-perler-29.bead-pattern.json`, `cat-perler-29_grid.png`, `cat-perler-29.pdf`; 667 beads, 11 colors, 580 × 580 grid PNG, 2 PDF pages.
- 2 × 2 boards: `cat-perler-58.bead-pattern.json`, `cat-perler-58_grid.png`, `cat-perler-58.pdf`; 2,726 beads, 13 colors, 1160 × 1160 grid PNG, 6 PDF pages.

Files live under `public/guides/photo-to-pattern/`. The project embeds the same
source JPEG bytes and enables replay through Open Project. The guide explicitly
uses this dark photograph as a difficult-input example: both results lose eye and
whisker details. They are practice files, not finished or physically tested portrait
patterns. PDF cells use palette symbols matched to the materials list. The exports do
not have the library PDFs' calibration line and must not be represented as verified
life-size pegboard templates.
